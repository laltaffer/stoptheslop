A deterministic 73-rule detector that reads your markup and names exactly how it looks generated: gradient-clipped headlines, fake dataviz, badge cards floated over the hero. It exits non-zero like it means it, and it catches tells that survive seven rounds of human critique. Skip its auto-fix; the check is the product.
